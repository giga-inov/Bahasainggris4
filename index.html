📖 English Learning Modules

📌 A. Daily Activities & Action Verbs (Kata Kerja Kegiatan)

Di Unit 1 ini, kita belajar menyatakan kegiatan yang sedang dilakukan (Present Continuous Tense).

📖 Reading Membaca
✍️ Writing Menulis
🍲 Eating Makan
🥛 Drinking Minum
😴 Sleeping Tidur
📝 Studying Belajar
⚽ Playing Bermain
📺 Watching TV Nonton TV
🍳 Cooking Memasak
🧼 Washing Mencuci

💬 Sentence Structure (Pola Kalimat):

Tanya: "What are you doing?" (Apa yang sedang kamu lakukan?)

Jawab: "I am reading a book." (Saya sedang membaca buku.)

Tanya: "What is he / she doing?" (Apa yang sedang dia lakukan?)

Jawab: "He is eating fried rice." / "She is studying English."

🔢 A. Numbers 50 to 100 & Counting Nouns

Mengenal angka puluhan dari 50 sampai 100 serta cara menghitung jumlah benda (Singular & Plural).

50: Fifty
60: Sixty
70: Seventy
80: Eighty
90: Ninety
100: One hundred

💡 Singular vs Plural Nouns (Benda Tunggal & Jamak):

  • 1 Benda (Singular): One book / One pencil
  • Banyak Benda (Plural): Tambahkan akhiran -s / -es ➔ Sixty-seven books / Fifty pencils.

➕ Math Operations in English:

"Twenty plus thirty is fifty" (20 + 30 = 50)

"Eighty minus twenty is sixty" (80 - 20 = 60)

🏡 A. Parts of the House & Furniture

Mengenal ruangan-ruangan di rumah, perabot (furniture), dan kata sifat (adjectives) penggambar kondisi ruangan.

🏠 Rooms in the House:

  • Living room: Ruang tamu
  • Bedroom: Kamar tidur
  • Kitchen: Dapur
  • Bathroom: Kamar mandi
  • Dining room: Ruang makan
  • Garage: Garasi

🛋️ Furniture & Objects:

  • Sofa: Sofa / Kursi empuk
  • Table & Chair: Meja & Kursi
  • Television: Televisi
  • Bed & Wardrobe: Tempat tidur & Lemari pakaian
  • Stove & Refrigerator: Kompor & Kulkas

✨ Adjectives (Kata Sifat):

Big (Besar) VS Small (Kecil) | Clean (Bersih) VS Dirty (Kotor) | Tidy (Rapi) VS Messy (Berantakan)

Contoh Kalimat: "My living room is big and clean." / "There is a big sofa in the living room."

🍳 A. Prepositions of Place & Room Activities

Menjelaskan letak posisi benda (Prepositions of Place) dan menggabungkannya dengan kegiatan harian di rumah.

In (Di dalam)
On (Di atas menempel)
Under (Di bawah)
Beside (Di samping)
Behind (Di belakang)
In front of (Di depan)
Between (Di antara dua benda)

🗣️ Example Conversations:

Q: "Where is Cici?" ➔ A: "She is in the kitchen."

Q: "What does she do in the kitchen?" ➔ A: "She cooks breakfast."
